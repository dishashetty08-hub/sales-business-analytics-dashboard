SALES & BUSINESS ANALYTICS DASHBOARD - POWER BI GUIDE



1. Open Power BI Desktop.

2. Get Data -> Excel -> select data/sales_data.xlsx -> Sales Data -> Load.

3. Create these measures:
Total Sales = SUM('Sales Data'[Sales])
                         
Total Profit = SUM('Sales Data'[Profit])
                         
Total Orders = COUNTROWS('Sales Data')
                         
Total Quantity = SUM('Sales Data'[Quantity])

4. Add KPI cards for the four measures.

5. Add a line chart: Date on X-axis and Total Sales as Values.

6. Add a column chart: Category and Total Sales.

7. Add a bar chart: Region and Total Sales.

8. Add a Top 10 Products chart using Product and Total Sales.

9. Add slicers for Date, Category and Region.

10. Save as powerbi/Sales_Dashboard.pbix.

Recommended title: Sales & Business Analytics Dashboard
